A target is not a guarantee.

Expected length decreases as the penalty increases. Its derivative is minus the length variance divided by temperature. Bisection uses that order, not a guessed Newton step.

Every legal path remains in the law. A positive penalty charges visited cells; a negative penalty rewards them. Neither is an accuracy improvement.

Length includes both endpoints. A single legal length makes the penalty unidentifiable, even when the target is met. Finite precision can also flatten a response. Endpoint matches are numerical, not proofs of an exact or unique parameter.

Every bracket decision

Each card is the pre-update bracket and its midpoint response. A mean above the target moves the lower penalty up; a mean below the target moves the upper penalty down.



Numerical evidence, not interval arithmetic.
Stable length partitions are computed once. Each penalty evaluation tilts their weights and computes the mean and centered variance. The complete-path oracle independently evaluates every witness penalty and verifies bracket decisions, stop conditions, selected response and status without calling the solver.
Stop at absolute mean residual 10⁻⁹ or pre-update penalty width 10⁻⁹, at most 40 midpoint evaluations. A width stop does not promise a mean residual of 10⁻⁹. Requests outside the finite endpoint range report the nearest sampled response, not a solution.
Binary64 rounding and underflow remain. This is a bounded scalar synthetic inverse problem, not a learned calibration model, a validated interval certificate, a benchmark or a novel algorithm.
